# Outlook Work Assistant – Your AI Productivity Partner for Outlook

**Outlook Work Assistant** by [Actor](https://actor.do) helps busy professionals manage their **emails, calendar, and tasks** directly inside Outlook.  
It’s a smart **AI productivity assistant** that understands your workflow, automates repetitive actions, and keeps your inbox and schedule organized.

---

## ✨ What You Can Do with Outlook Work Assistant

- 🧩 **Smart email management** — label emails, extract tasks, and generate drafts with one click.  
- 🗓️ **Calendar integration** — automatically summarize meetings and prepare your day.  
- ✅ **Task follow-ups** — never lose track of emails that require action.  
- ⚡ **AI-powered replies** — write better, faster, and more context-aware responses.  
- 🧠 **Context memory** — your assistant learns your preferences and adapts to your workflow.
